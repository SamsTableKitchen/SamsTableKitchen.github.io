Best Burgers

Bacon, runny eggs and a half-pound garlic burger: the burgers I'd drive across town (or to Zion) for.

How I pick: only places I've actually eaten at and reviewed here, ranked by my own rating. No sponsored spots. Ties are listed in the order I'd send a friend.
  1. #1

    Fuku Burger Chinatown

    Las Vegas, NV · ★★★★★ 5/5

    Great burgers and fries in Chinatown. The bacon-loaded BUTA and the TAMAGO with a runny egg are the ones to get.

    Read my review →Google Maps

  2. #2

    Slater's 50/50

    Las Vegas, NV · ★★★★★ 5/5

    A relaxed spot off the Strip with excellent burgers, standout crispy bacon, and fried mac and cheese balls that are crispy outside and creamy inside.

    Read my review →Google Maps

  3. #3

    Oscar's Cafe

    Springdale, UT · ★★★★★ 5/5

    Five minutes from Zion, the half-pound garlic Murder Burger was one of the best burgers I've ever had, at a great price.
