Best Places to Eat in Alaska

Alaska surprised me with some of the best Thai, pizza and fusion food I've had anywhere.

How I pick: only places I've actually eaten at and reviewed here, ranked by my own rating. No sponsored spots. Ties are listed in the order I'd send a friend.
  1. #1

    Seoul Casa

    Anchorage, AK · ★★★★★ 5/5

    Korean and Mexican flavors done right: crispy bulgogi egg rolls and tender kalbi beef enchiladas, with mac salad on the side.

    Read my review →Google Maps

  2. #2

    Thai Alaska Kitchen

    Sitka, AK · ★★★★★ 5/5

    Some of the best Thai food I've had in Alaska. The panang curry is rich and flavorful, served with beautiful butterfly pea flower rice.

    Read my review →Google Maps

  3. #3

    Uncle Joe's Pizzeria

    Anchorage, AK · ★★★★★ 5/5

    Phenomenal stone-baked pizza, like the Gyro Pizza, with friendly, attentive service and a warm, welcoming room.
