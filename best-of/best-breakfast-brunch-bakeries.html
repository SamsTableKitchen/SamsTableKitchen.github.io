Best Breakfast, Brunch & Bakeries

Pancakes, chocolate croissants and fresh Cuban bread: my favorite ways to start the day.

How I pick: only places I've actually eaten at and reviewed here, ranked by my own rating. No sponsored spots. Ties are listed in the order I'd send a friend.
  1. #1

    Hash House A Go Go (Plaza)

    Las Vegas, NV · ★★★★★ 5/5

    Great food and friendly, attentive service downtown. The fried chicken eggs Benedict and a pancake to share made it an easy one to recommend.

    Read my review →Google Maps

  2. #2

    Bonrue Bakery

    Hurricane, UT · ★★★★★ 5/5

    A must-stop on the way to Zion. The pastry case is gorgeous, and a striped chocolate croissant with coffee is the perfect start to a hike day.

    Read my review →Google Maps

  3. #3

    La Segunda Bakery and Cafe

    Tampa, FL · ★★★★★ 5/5

    Home of the best Cuban sandwich I've had in Florida, on bread with a perfect crackle, and a pastry case stacked with cream puffs, turnovers and cakes.

    Read my review →Google Maps
