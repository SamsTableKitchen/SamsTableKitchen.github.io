My meal prep basics

  • Cool, then cover. Let food cool before closing the lid so it doesn't get soggy.
  • Most meals keep 3 to 4 days. For Thursday and Friday, freeze portions on Sunday and move them to the fridge the night before.
  • Pack crunchy and fresh things separately. Greens, avocado, sauces, and tortillas stay better on the side.
  • Reheat until steaming hot, and stir halfway through in the microwave.
