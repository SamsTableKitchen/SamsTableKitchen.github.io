Restaurant Review · Lake Havasu City, AZ

PZA Pizzeria

Wood-Fired, Build-Your-Own Pizza

★★★★★Rated 5 out of 5
PZA Pizzeria
1641 McCulloch Blvd N, Lake Havasu City, AZ 86403
Rating: 5/5

I recently visited a pizza place that offers a fantastic wood-fired, build-your-own pizza experience, and I was thoroughly impressed. The freshness of the ingredients really stood out, and watching them shape the dough by hand was a cool touch. Every bite tasted incredibly fresh and flavorful.

One of the standout pizzas for me was the BLT. It didn't have any cheese, which I thought might be a downside at first, but it turned out to be my favorite! The combination of bacon jam, arugula, and fresh tomatoes was so delicious. The savory and slightly sweet bacon jam paired perfectly with the peppery arugula, and the tomatoes added a refreshing balance with the balsamic vinegar glaze. If you're into unique pizza flavors, I highly recommend giving it a try. Definitely want to come back and try the Mac and cheese pizza.

What we ordered

BLT Pizza · Build-Your-Own Pizza (ham and black olive)

About the place

Locally owned, quick wood-fired pizzeria on McCulloch Blvd. Dough is slow fermented and hand stretched, and the Neapolitan-style personal pizzas bake in about 90 seconds. You can order from the menu or build your own from 50+ toppings, with gluten-free and cauliflower crusts, salads, wood-fired subs and craft beer. Specialties include the BLT, the Mac and Cheese pizza and dessert pizzas.

What other diners say: Reviewers on Google (summarized by Restaurantji) often praise the crust, the fresh toppings and the build-your-own setup, along with friendly, quick service and fair prices. The BLT pizza with bacon jam gets mentioned often.

Sources: PZA's website, Go Lake Havasu and Restaurantji. Summaries are paraphrased, not quotes.
