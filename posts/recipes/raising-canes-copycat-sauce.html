Raising Cane's Copycat Sauce

Recipe by Kari at Southern Bytes.

Prep time5 min (plus 6 hr chilling)
TotalAbout 6 hr 5 min
Serves4

Times are estimates based on the steps.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 1/4 cup ketchup
  • 1/4 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon coarse ground black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon Tony Chachere's Creole Seasoning

Steps

  1. Put the mayo, ketchup, Worcestershire, pepper, garlic powder, and Creole seasoning in a small bowl.
  2. Mix until smooth, then move it to a jar or airtight container.
  3. Chill at least 6 hours before serving. Overnight (12–24 hours) tastes best. Right away it just tastes like ketchup.
  4. Keep it in the fridge and use within 5–7 days. Don't freeze it.
My tip

For a healthier option, swap out half the mayo for plain Greek yogurt.
