Bread Machine Thin Crust Pizza Dough

Let the bread machine do the work for pizza night.

Bread Machine Thin Crust Pizza Dough

Recipe by Healthy Boy at Allrecipes.

Prep timeAbout 1 hr 10 min (mostly machine time)
Pre-bake5 min
Total1 hr 15 min
Makes1 (14-inch) crust

Times are estimates based on the steps.

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup warm water (100–110°F)
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon active dry yeast
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon white sugar
  • 2 teaspoons olive oil

Steps

  1. Pour the warm water into the bread machine pan. Add the flour on top, then sprinkle in the yeast, salt, and sugar.
  2. Set the machine to the dough setting and press start.
  3. When it's done, move the dough to a well-floured counter. Heat the oven to 425°F.
  4. Roll or stretch the dough into a thin 14-inch circle, leaving the edge a little thicker. Put it on a pizza pan and brush with olive oil.
  5. Pre-bake for 5 minutes. Take it out, add your toppings, and finish baking until the crust is golden and the toppings are hot.

Notes

Try it with my slow cooker marinara as the sauce.
