English Muffins

Recipe by Bob Dickinson.

🧪 Kitchen-test in progress: I haven't made this one yet, so let me know how it turns out!

Prep time25 min (plus 1 hr rise)
Cook timeAbout 30 min (in batches)
MakesAbout 10 to 12 muffins (approximate)

Ingredients

  • 550 g (about 4½ cups) bread flour
  • 1 tablespoon caster sugar (superfine sugar)
  • 7 g (2¼ teaspoons, 1 packet) dried yeast
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 350 ml (about 1½ cups) whole milk
  • Semolina, for dusting

Steps

  1. Warm the milk: Warm the milk in a small pan over low heat until just warm, not hot. If your yeast needs activating, stir it into 50 ml (about 3 tablespoons) of the warm milk and let it sit 5 to 10 minutes until foamy.
  2. Make the dough: Combine the flour, sugar, yeast and salt in a large bowl. Add the warm milk (and the bloomed yeast, if used) and mix to a dough. Knead for 10 minutes until smooth and elastic.
  3. Roll and cut: Roll the dough out on a floured surface to about 2 cm (¾ inch) thick, or thinner for thinner muffins. Cut rounds with a 7.5 cm (3 inch) cutter, re-rolling the scraps until all the dough is used.
  4. Rise: Dust a tray with semolina and space the muffins so they don't touch. Dust the tops with semolina, cover loosely, and let them rise in a warm place for 1 hour or until doubled.
  5. Cook: Heat a large dry skillet over medium heat (no oil needed). Cook the muffins in batches for about 7 minutes per side. Turn the heat down if the bottoms brown too fast.
  6. Cool: Cool the muffins on a wire rack before splitting and toasting.

Pro tips

  • Check doneness: The sides should feel springy, and the center should read about 200°F (93°C).
  • Split, don't slice: Pull them apart with a fork to keep the nooks and crannies.
