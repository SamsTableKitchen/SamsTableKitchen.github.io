Bob Evans-Style Mac and Cheese (Copycat)

Recipe by Audrey Wood at Two Pink Peonies.

Prep time5 min
Cook time20 min
Total25 min
Serves10

Times are estimates based on the steps.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups cooked elbow macaroni
  • 1/2 cup shredded mild or medium cheddar
  • 16 oz Velveeta
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 3/4 teaspoon paprika
  • 1 teaspoon dry mustard
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Steps

  1. Cook the macaroni according to the package and drain.
  2. Cut the Velveeta into small cubes.
  3. Spray a large pot with nonstick spray. Add the Velveeta, cheddar, and milk.
  4. Cook over low heat, stirring, until it's all melted and smooth.
  5. Stir in the paprika, dry mustard, salt, and pepper.
  6. Stir in the pasta and serve.

Notes

This is a homemade copycat-style recipe. It isn't affiliated with Bob Evans.

Some cheeses are made with animal rennet. Check the labels if you eat strictly vegetarian.

Original recipe: Copycat Bob Evans Mac and Cheese, Two Pink Peonies. Steps rewritten in my own words.
