Buttermilk Fried Chicken Tenders

Adapted from Billy Parisi.

Need it dairy-free? Try my Dairy-Free Southern Fried Chicken Tenders.

Prep time20 min (plus 2–24 hr marinating)
Cook timeAbout 16 min (in batches)
TotalAbout 2 hr 40 min
Serves6 to 8

Ingredients

  • 2 lb chicken tenders (or chicken breasts cut into strips)
  • Marinade:
  • 1 1/2 cups buttermilk
  • 1/4 cup pickle juice (or white distilled vinegar)
  • 1 tablespoon hot sauce
  • 1 1/2 tablespoons garlic granules
  • 1 1/2 tablespoons onion granules
  • 2 teaspoons paprika
  • 2 teaspoons coarse salt
  • 1 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • Breading:
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup cornstarch
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • 2 teaspoons garlic granules
  • 2 teaspoons onion granules
  • 2 teaspoons paprika
  • 3 teaspoons coarse salt, divided (2 for the flour, 1 for the egg wash)
  • 1 teaspoon ground black pepper, divided
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup buttermilk
  • Oil for frying (vegetable, canola, or peanut)

Steps

  1. Stir all the marinade ingredients together in a large bowl, add the chicken, cover, and refrigerate 2 to 24 hours. Longer gives more flavor and more tender meat.
  2. In a wide, shallow dish, whisk the flour, cornstarch, baking powder, garlic and onion granules, paprika, 2 teaspoons salt, and 1/2 teaspoon pepper.
  3. In a second shallow dish, whisk the eggs and buttermilk with the last 1 teaspoon salt and 1/2 teaspoon pepper.
  4. Fill a 4- to 5-quart pot halfway with oil and bring it to 375°F over medium-low heat. Adding the cold chicken will drop it to about 350°F, which is right where you want it.
  5. Working a few at a time, lift tenders from the marinade and let the extra drip off. Press into the flour on all sides, dip fully in the egg wash, let it drip, then press into the flour a second time.
  6. Set the coated tenders on a wire rack over a sheet pan and let them rest up to 15 minutes while you coat the rest. This helps the crust stick.
  7. Fry 4 to 6 at a time for 3 1/2 to 4 minutes, turning every 2 minutes, until deep golden and cooked through (165°F inside). Don't crowd the pot.
  8. Drain on a wire rack, not paper towels, while you fry the remaining batches. Serve with ranch, blue cheese, or honey mustard.

Notes

  • Oven option: Arrange the coated tenders on a rack set over a parchment-lined sheet pan, spray the tops with cooking spray, and bake at 425°F for 18–22 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden and cooked through.
  • Keep warm: Hold the rack of fried tenders in a 200°F oven for up to 30 minutes.
  • Leftovers: Refrigerate up to 4 days in an airtight container with a paper towel underneath. Reheat in a 375°F oven or air fryer until crisp.
  • Throw out the used marinade.

Adapted from Fried Chicken Tenders, Chef Billy Parisi. Steps rewritten in my own words.
