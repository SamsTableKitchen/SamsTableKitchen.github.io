Huli Huli Chicken

Recipe by Joe Duff at I'm Hungry For That.

Prep time3 hr (mostly marinating)
Cook time15 min
Total3 hr 15 min
Makes6 servings

Ingredients

  • 4 pounds boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • 1 cup pineapple juice
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup ketchup
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 2 teaspoons fresh ginger root, grated
  • 2 teaspoons minced garlic
  • Sesame seeds
  • Green onions, sliced

Steps

  1. Whisk the pineapple juice, soy sauce, brown sugar, ketchup, ginger, and garlic. Save 1 cup for later.
  2. Marinate the chicken in the rest for at least 3 hours or overnight.
  3. Heat the grill to medium and oil it lightly with sesame oil.
  4. Grill 6–8 minutes per side until golden (about 170°F inside). After flipping, brush on the saved marinade.
  5. Top with sesame seeds and green onions.

Original recipe: Huli Huli Chicken, I'm Hungry For That. Steps rewritten in my own words.
