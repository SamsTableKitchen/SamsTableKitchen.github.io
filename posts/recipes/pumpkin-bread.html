Pumpkin Bread

Adapted from Natasha Kravchuk of Natasha’s Kitchen banana bread.

Prep time15 min
Bake time55–65 min
Total1 hour 20 min
Makes2 loaves

Ingredients

  • 2 cups pure canned pumpkin puree
  • ½ cup unsalted butter, softened
  • ½ cup neutral oil (canola or vegetable)
  • 1½ cups granulated sugar
  • ½ cup packed brown sugar
  • 4 large eggs, lightly beaten
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 3–3¼ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
  • ½ teaspoon ground nutmeg
  • ½ teaspoon ground ginger
  • ¼ teaspoon ground cloves
  • 1 cup semisweet chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease and flour two standard loaf pans.
  2. Optional: blot the pumpkin puree on paper towels for a minute if it looks watery.
  3. Cream the butter with both sugars until light and fluffy, then beat in the oil.
  4. Mix in the eggs, vanilla, and pumpkin puree until smooth.
  5. In a separate bowl, whisk the flour, baking soda, baking powder, salt, cinnamon, nutmeg, ginger, and cloves.
  6. Stir the dry ingredients into the wet just until combined.
  7. Toss the chocolate chips in 1 tablespoon of the flour reserved from the measured amount, then fold them into the batter so they don't sink.
  8. Divide the batter between the pans.
  9. Bake 55–65 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean or the center reaches 200–205°F (93–96°C). Start checking at 55 minutes.
  10. Cool in the pans 10 minutes, then move to a wire rack.

Notes

Start with 3 cups of flour and add the extra ¼ cup only if the batter looks very loose.

Use pure pumpkin puree, not pumpkin pie filling. One 15 oz can is just under 2 cups, which is all you need.

I left out the walnuts and raisins and added chocolate chips.

I use half butter and half oil so the loaves stay moist for days.

I'm planning to test a version with only baking soda later.

Adapted from Natasha Kravchuk of Natasha’s Kitchen banana bread.
