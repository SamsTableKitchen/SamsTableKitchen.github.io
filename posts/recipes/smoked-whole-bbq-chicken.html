Smoked Whole BBQ Chicken

🚧 Work in progress My own smoked chicken. It starts with my Chicken or Turkey Brine.

Cook temp325°F
BrineOvernight, up to 24 hr
Pull temp160°F

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken
  • Chicken or Turkey Brine (1 cup mix in ½ gallon water)
  • Your favorite BBQ seasoning
  • BBQ sauce

Steps

  1. Brine: Brine the chicken overnight, or up to 24 hours, in the Chicken or Turkey Brine. Rinse and pat dry.
  2. Spatchcock: Spatchcock the chicken.
  3. Season: Season with your favorite BBQ seasoning.
  4. Cook: Smoke on a pellet grill, or cook in the oven, at 325°F until the thickest part of the breast reaches 155°F.
  5. Sauce and finish: Brush with BBQ sauce and keep cooking until about 160°F. Pull it at 160°F to keep it from drying out; carryover heat brings it to 165°F as it rests.

Pro tips

  • Wings getting dark? If the wings get too dark while cooking, wrap them in foil (optional).
