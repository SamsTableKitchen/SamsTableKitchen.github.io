Tzatziki Sauce

Recipe by Jeanine Donofrio at Love and Lemons.

Prep time5 min
Total5 min
Makes4 to 6 servings

Ingredients

  • ½ cup finely grated cucumber
  • 1 cup thick whole milk Greek yogurt
  • 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
  • ½ tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 garlic clove, grated
  • ¼ teaspoon sea salt
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh dill
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh mint (optional)

Steps

  1. Squeeze some of the water out of the grated cucumber with a towel.
  2. Stir everything together in a bowl.
  3. Chill until you're ready to eat.

Original recipe: Tzatziki Sauce, Love and Lemons. Steps rewritten in my own words.
