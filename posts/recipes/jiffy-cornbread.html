The Best Jiffy Cornbread

Recipe by Christin Mahrlig at Spicy Southern Kitchen.

Prep time5 min
Cook time25–30 min
TotalAbout 30 min
Makes12 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 2/3 cup milk
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup butter, melted
  • 2 boxes Jiffy corn muffin mix
  • 1 tablespoon sugar

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F and grease a 9×13-inch pan.
  2. Whisk the sour cream, milk, and eggs, then whisk in the butter.
  3. Stir in the muffin mix and sugar and pour into the pan.
  4. Bake 25–30 minutes until golden. Serve warm.

Notes

  • Leftovers make great croutons: cube, toss with butter, and bake at 350°F until crisp.

Original recipe: The Best Jiffy Cornbread, Spicy Southern Kitchen. Steps rewritten in my own words.
