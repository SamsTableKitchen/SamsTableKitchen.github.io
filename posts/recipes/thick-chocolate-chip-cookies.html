Thick Chocolate Chip Cookies

Recipe by Bob Dickinson.

🧪 Kitchen-test in progress: I haven't made this one yet, so let me know how it turns out!

Prep time15 min (plus 2 hr freezing)
Bake time15 to 20 min
Makes9 large cookies

Ingredients

  • 200 g (about ¾ cup + 2 tablespoons) butter, melted
  • 200 g (about 1 cup packed) light brown sugar
  • 50 g (about ¼ cup) caster sugar (superfine sugar)
  • 2 eggs
  • 400 g (about 3⅓ cups) self-raising flour (see note)
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 350 g (about 12 oz) dark chocolate, cut into chunks
  • Flaky sea salt

Steps

  1. Mix the butter and sugars: Stir the melted butter, brown sugar and caster sugar together in a large bowl until combined.
  2. Add the eggs and vanilla: Beat in the eggs and vanilla until smooth.
  3. Add the flour: Fold in the flour until a rough dough forms.
  4. Add the chocolate: Add the chocolate chunks and a pinch of flaky sea salt, and mix until just combined.
  5. Shape: Roll the dough into 140 g (about 5 oz) balls.
  6. Freeze: Freeze the dough balls for 2 hours, or until frozen. This keeps the cookies thick.
  7. Bake: Heat the oven to 180°C / 350°F. Bake the frozen dough balls on a lined tray, spaced well apart, for 15 to 20 minutes until lightly golden.
  8. Cool: Let the cookies cool on the tray before eating, since they firm up as they cool.

Pro tips

  • No self-raising flour? Use 400 g (about 3⅓ cups) all-purpose flour plus 4 teaspoons baking powder and ½ teaspoon salt.
  • Bake from frozen: Keep extra dough balls in the freezer and bake a few at a time.
