Yellow Curry with Seared Bone-In Chicken Thighs & Drumsticks

Adapted from the Yellow Curry recipe at Tastes Better From Scratch.

Prep time15 min
Cook time45–55 min
Total60–70 min
Serves3–4
Oven temp350°F (175°C)
MethodSear + covered bake

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • 2 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs + 2 drumsticks (about 1½–2 lb total, from a whole chicken cut 8 ways)
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 1–2 tbsp oil (for searing)
  • Curry Base
  • ½ large onion, chopped
  • 2 medium gold or Yukon potatoes, cut into ~½-inch cubes
  • 1 large carrot, thinly sliced
  • 3–4 tbsp yellow curry paste (Mae Ploy recommended)
  • 2 tsp freshly grated ginger
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 27 oz full-fat coconut milk (two 13.5 oz cans)
  • 2 tsp cornstarch
  • Finish & Serve
  • 1 tsp fish sauce (optional but recommended)
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • 1½ tbsp brown sugar
  • Hot cooked rice (jasmine or white) for serving
  • Fresh cilantro and/or sriracha, plus lime wedges for garnish

Steps

  1. Preheat and prep. Heat oven to 350°F (175°C). Pat the thighs and drumsticks very dry, then season generously with salt and pepper on all sides.
  2. Sear the chicken. Heat the oil in a large oven-safe skillet or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Sear the chicken skin-side down until deeply golden, about 5–7 minutes. Flip and sear the other side 2–3 minutes. Transfer to a plate. Work in batches if needed to avoid crowding.
  3. Build the curry base. Lower heat to medium. In the same pan, sauté the onion in the rendered fat for a few minutes until softened. Add the potatoes and carrots and cook 1–2 minutes. Stir in the ginger, garlic, and yellow curry paste; cook 2–3 minutes, stirring, to bloom the spices (this step is key for flavor).
  4. Add the coconut milk. Pour in 1½ cans of the coconut milk. Whisk the cornstarch into the remaining ½ can until smooth, then stir it into the pan. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook 2–3 minutes so the sauce starts to thicken slightly.
  5. Oven finish. Nestle the seared chicken back into the sauce (skin-side up if possible), spooning some sauce over the tops. Cover tightly with a lid or foil. Transfer to the oven and bake 30–40 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through (165°F / 74°C in the thickest part near the bone) and the potatoes are tender. Dark meat can go a bit higher without drying out.
  6. Finish. Remove from the oven. Stir in the fish sauce, brown sugar, and lime juice. Taste and adjust (more lime for brightness, sugar for balance, or a splash of water if the sauce is too thick). Rest 5 minutes.
  7. Serve. Spoon over rice and garnish with cilantro and/or a drizzle of sriracha. Extra lime wedges on the side are nice.

Notes

Skin: Leaving the skin on during searing adds flavor. It will soften in the sauce but still tastes great. Remove it after searing if you prefer skinless.

Sauce thickness: The cornstarch gives body. If it thickens too much in the oven, loosen with a little water or extra coconut milk when you stir in the finishers.

Dutch oven note: 350°F covered is a standard, reliable temperature for seared bone-in dark meat. The heavy lid traps heat and moisture. Start checking at 30 minutes and use a thermometer.

Make-ahead / leftovers: Keeps well for 3–4 days. Store sauce and rice separately if possible. Reheat gently.

Variations: Extra veggies (bell pepper, zucchini, bamboo shoots) work well. Start with 3 tbsp paste for milder, 4 tbsp for more heat.

Pan: An oven-safe skillet or Dutch oven is ideal. If yours isn't oven-safe, transfer everything to a baking dish after building the sauce.

Adapted from the Yellow Curry recipe at Tastes Better From Scratch. I adapted this for bone-in thighs and drumsticks with a sear-then-oven finish.
