Fried Yardbird and Chicken Shake

Recipe by Marcus Samuelsson, from The Red Rooster Cookbook, at marcussamuelsson.com.

Start the day before. The chicken marinates overnight.

Prep time30 min
Brine and marinate1 1/2 hr plus overnight
Cook timeAbout 30 min (in batches)
TotalAbout 10 1/2 hr, mostly hands-off
Makes8 pieces

Times are estimates based on the steps.

Ingredients

  • For the Chicken Shake (makes about 1 cup):
  • 1/4 cup berbere
  • 1/4 cup hot smoked paprika
  • 2 tablespoons ground cumin
  • 2 tablespoons freshly ground white pepper
  • 2 tablespoons celery salt
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons granulated garlic
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons coarse kosher salt
  • For the chicken:
  • 8 cups water
  • 1 cup coarse kosher salt
  • 4 chicken thighs
  • 4 chicken drumsticks
  • 2 cups buttermilk
  • 3/4 cup coconut milk
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tablespoon Chicken Shake, plus more for serving
  • Peanut oil, for frying
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup semolina flour
  • 2 tablespoons cornstarch
  • 1 tablespoon freshly ground white pepper

Steps

  1. Make the Chicken Shake: whisk the berbere, smoked paprika, cumin, white pepper, celery salt, granulated garlic, and kosher salt together. Store it in a jar away from light.
  2. Brine the chicken: heat 2 cups of the water with the 1 cup salt over high heat until it simmers and the salt dissolves. Pour it into a large container, add the other 6 cups of water, and let it cool to room temperature. Add the chicken, cover, and refrigerate for 1 1/2 hours. Drain.
  3. Marinate: whisk the buttermilk, coconut milk, garlic, and 1 tablespoon Chicken Shake in a 9x13-inch baking dish. Put the chicken in so it's covered, cover the dish, and refrigerate overnight.
  4. The next day, fill a large saucepan one-third full with peanut oil. Heat it over medium-high to 360°F.
  5. While the oil heats, whisk the flour, semolina, cornstarch, and white pepper in a bowl. Let the extra marinade drip off each piece, then roll it in the flour, pressing the coating on. Set the pieces on a rack over a baking sheet. If the coating looks wet, roll it in the flour again.
  6. Fry in batches until deep brown and 165°F inside, about 10 minutes per batch. Adjust the heat to keep the oil between 350°F and 375°F. Drain on a rack over a baking sheet.
  7. Sprinkle with more Chicken Shake and serve.

Notes

Leftover Chicken Shake keeps in a jar, out of the light, for up to 6 months.

Original recipe: Fried Yardbird and Chicken Shake from The Red Rooster Cookbook, marcussamuelsson.com. Steps rewritten in my own words.
