Coconut Red Lentil Dal

Recipe by The Plant Slant (watch the video).

Serve it with my Vegan Naan for scooping.

Prep time10 min
Cook timeAbout 40 min
TotalAbout 50 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 3 teaspoons (1 tablespoon) cumin seeds
  • 2 onions, finely sliced
  • 4 cloves garlic, finely sliced
  • 1 inch piece fresh ginger, peeled and finely sliced
  • 1 teaspoon ground turmeric
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • 200 g (about 1 cup) red lentils, rinsed
  • 1 can (400 g / 14 oz) chopped tomatoes
  • 1 can (400 ml / 13.5–14 oz) coconut milk
  • 150 ml (about 2/3 cup) just-boiled water, plus 50–100 ml (3–7 tablespoons) more if needed
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • A small handful of cilantro (coriander) leaves, finely chopped
  • Sea salt
  • Cooked rice, to serve

Steps

  1. Rinse the lentils in a sieve under cold water until the water runs mostly clear. Drain and set aside.
  2. Heat the oil in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Add the cumin seeds and let them sizzle and crackle for a few seconds.
  3. Add the onions and cook, stirring now and then, for 8–10 minutes until soft and golden.
  4. Add the garlic and ginger and stir constantly for about 30 seconds, until fragrant.
  5. Stir in the turmeric, chili powder, lentils, tomatoes, coconut milk and a good pinch of salt. Pour in the 150 ml (2/3 cup) just-boiled water and mix well.
  6. Bring to a boil, then cover, turn the heat to low and simmer for 25–30 minutes, stirring now and then so it doesn't stick, until the lentils are soft and broken down. If it gets too thick, stir in another 50–100 ml (3–7 tablespoons) water.
  7. Take the pan off the heat and stir in the lime juice and most of the cilantro. Taste and add more salt if needed.
  8. Serve over rice, topped with the rest of the cilantro.

Notes

  • Heat level: 1 teaspoon chili powder gives a gentle-to-medium heat. Use less for mild, or more if you like it spicy.
  • Texture: Dal thickens as it sits. Loosen leftovers with a splash of water when reheating.
  • Leftovers: Keeps 4 days in the fridge or 3 months in the freezer.
  • Naturally vegan, dairy-free and gluten-free (serve with rice).
