High-Protein Tuna Salad

Crunchy, tangy tuna salad made lighter with Greek yogurt.

High-Protein Tuna Salad

Adapted from a Reddit post on r/LowCalorieCooking.

🧪 Kitchen-test in progress: I haven't made this one yet, so let me know how it turns out!

Prep time10 min
Chill15 min
ServesAbout 2

Ingredients

  • Salad:
  • 1 can tuna, drained
  • ½ green onion, sliced
  • 2 mini cucumbers, diced
  • ½ large or 1 small red onion, diced
  • 2 tablespoons fresh dill, chopped (optional)
  • Optional add-ins: chickpeas or chopped pickles
  • Dressing:
  • 100 g (about ⅓ cup) 0% Greek yogurt
  • 40 g (about 3 tablespoons) light mayo
  • 1 tablespoon mustard
  • 1 small tablespoon lime juice
  • Black pepper, paprika, chili flakes and salt, to taste

Steps

  1. Prep the vegetables: Dice the cucumbers and red onion, and slice the green onion. Chop the dill if using.
  2. Make the dressing: Whisk the Greek yogurt, mayo, mustard and lime juice in a bowl. Season with pepper, paprika, chili flakes and salt.
  3. Combine: Add the drained tuna, vegetables and any add-ins, and fold everything together gently.
  4. Taste and adjust: Add more lime, salt or chili flakes if needed.
  5. Chill and serve: Chill for at least 15 minutes. Serve in lettuce wraps, on toast, or with crackers.

Pro tips

  • Drain well: Press the tuna dry so the salad doesn't get watery.
  • Meal prep: Keeps covered in the fridge for up to 2 days.
