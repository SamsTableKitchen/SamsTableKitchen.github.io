Vegan Naan

Recipe by Andrew Bernard at Make It Dairy Free.

Great with my Coconut Red Lentil Dal.

Prep time10 min
Rise time1 to 1 1/2 hr
Cook timeAbout 15 min
TotalAbout 1 hr 25 min
Makes10 to 12 naan

Ingredients

  • 3 1/2 to 4 cups flour (bread flour is best; all-purpose or whole wheat also work)
  • 1 packet (2 1/4 teaspoons) instant yeast
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 1/2 cups dairy-free milk, warmed to about 110°F (oat or another mild milk; water works in a pinch)
  • 1/4 cup maple syrup (or agave or vegan cane sugar)
  • 1/4 cup vegan butter, melted, for brushing
  • About 1/2 cup water, for brushing the dough

Steps

  1. In a large bowl, whisk 3 1/2 cups of the flour with the yeast and salt.
  2. Pour in the warm dairy-free milk and maple syrup. Fold with a spatula until mostly combined, adding a little more flour if needed until it forms a soft ball that isn't sticky.
  3. Lightly oil the same bowl, return the dough, cover with a towel or plastic wrap, and let it rise in a warm spot for 60–90 minutes, until doubled.
  4. Turn the dough out, shape it into a ball, and divide into 10–12 pieces. Roll each into a ball, then flatten with your hands or a rolling pin into a round or teardrop shape about 1/4 inch thick.
  5. Heat a cast iron skillet over medium-high heat. Lightly brush both sides of a piece of dough with water.
  6. Cook about 1 minute until bubbles form and the bottom has golden spots, then flip and cook about 1 minute more.
  7. Brush with melted vegan butter and keep warm in a towel-lined bowl, covered. Repeat with the rest of the dough and serve warm.

Notes

  • Milk temperature: Warm, not hot. Around 110°F (comfortably warm to the touch) wakes up the yeast; too hot will kill it.
  • Oil-free: Flour the bowl instead of oiling it.
  • Leftovers: Wrap and keep at room temperature 2 days, or freeze. Reheat in a dry skillet.

Adapted from Vegan Naan, Andrew Bernard at Make It Dairy Free. Steps rewritten in my own words.
