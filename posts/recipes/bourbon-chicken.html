Bourbon Chicken

Adapted from a recipe shared on Reddit.

Prep time15 min
Cook time40 min
Total55 min

Times are estimates based on the steps.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • White pepper, salt, and MSG to season
  • Sauce:
  • 1 cup soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1 1/2 cups brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 2-3 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
  • 1/4 cup bourbon
  • 2-3 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, chopped
  • To finish:
  • Toasted sesame seeds
  • Chopped green onions

Steps

  1. Cut the chicken into bite-size pieces. Season with white pepper, salt, and MSG.
  2. Make the sauce. In a large pot over medium heat, cook the garlic and ginger for 2–3 minutes.
  3. Add the rest of the sauce ingredients and stir until the sugar dissolves. Bring to a boil, then turn the heat down and simmer about 5 minutes to cook off some of the alcohol.
  4. Add the chicken to the sauce. Let it simmer, stirring now and then, until the chicken reaches 185°F inside. Thighs stay juicy at this temperature.
  5. Check a few of the thickest pieces with a meat thermometer.
  6. Take the chicken out of the pot with a slotted spoon and set it aside in a dish.
  7. If the sauce is thin, keep simmering it in the pot until it thickens, about 10–15 minutes.
  8. Put the chicken back in the pot and toss to coat it in the sauce.
  9. Top with toasted sesame seeds and chopped green onions. Serve with rice and broccoli if desired.
My tip

Once the chicken hits 185°F, I take it out of the pot before I reduce the sauce. Simmering the sauce on its own thickens it fast without overcooking the chicken. Then I add the chicken back and toss to coat.
