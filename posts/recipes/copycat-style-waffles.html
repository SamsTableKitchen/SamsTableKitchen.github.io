Waffle House-Style Waffles (Copycat)

Recipe by Brendan Cunningham at I'm Hungry For That.

Prep time10 min
Cook time10 min
Total20 min
Makes8 waffles

Times are estimates based on the steps.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup cornstarch
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 large egg, lightly beaten
  • 1/3 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter, softened, plus more for the iron and serving
  • 3/4 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 1/2 cup buttermilk
  • Berries and maple syrup, for serving

Steps

  1. Stir the flour, cornstarch, salt, baking soda, and baking powder together in a large bowl.
  2. In another bowl, whisk the egg, sugar, butter, and vanilla until smooth. Whisk in the milk and buttermilk.
  3. Gently fold the dry ingredients into the wet ones until just mixed. A few lumps are fine.
  4. Heat your waffle iron. Brush it with a little butter and add about 1/2 cup batter.
  5. Cook 4–6 minutes, until golden and crispy. Serve right away with butter, berries, and syrup.

Notes

This is a homemade copycat-style recipe. It isn't affiliated with Waffle House.

Original recipe: Copycat Waffle House Waffles Recipe, I'm Hungry For That. Steps rewritten in my own words.
