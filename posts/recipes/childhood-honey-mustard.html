Childhood Honey Mustard

Sauces

Childhood Honey Mustard

A Sam's Table original.

Prep time5 min
Total5 min

Times are estimates based on the steps.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup Miracle Whip
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 2 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 3 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tsp lemon juice

Steps

  1. Whisk everything together until smooth and the brown sugar dissolves.
  2. Chill before serving if you like it thicker.
