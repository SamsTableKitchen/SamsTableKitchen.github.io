Pellet Grill Frozen Chicken Wings (0 to 400 Method)

A Sam's Table original.

Prep time5 min
Cook timeAbout 1 hr
TotalAbout 1 hr 5 min

Times are estimates based on the steps.

Ingredients

  • Small frozen chicken wings
  • Oil
  • Your favorite seasoning or rub
  • Wing sauce (optional)

Steps

  1. Toss the frozen wings with oil and seasoning.
  2. Put them on a cold grill and set it to Smoke for about 10 minutes.
  3. Raise the heat to 400°F and cook 30 minutes.
  4. Flip, then keep cooking in about 10-minute rounds, flipping and checking the temperature each time.
  5. Pull the wings at 185–190°F inside. Toss in sauce if you like.
My tip

Wings are safe at 165°F, but taking them higher to 185–190°F makes them tender with crispy skin.
