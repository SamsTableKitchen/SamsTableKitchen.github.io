BBQ Chicken Drumsticks

My pellet-grill BBQ drumsticks. I brine them overnight in my Chicken or Turkey Brine.

Smoke300°F · 45 min–1 hr + 10 min
Oven425°F · about 40–50 min
Pull temp165°F

Ingredients

  • About 10–12 chicken drumsticks
  • Chicken or Turkey Brine (1 cup mix per gallon of water)
  • BBQ sauce of your choice

Steps

  1. Brine: I brine the drumsticks for 24 hours in the Chicken or Turkey Brine, using 1 cup of brine mix per gallon of water. Then I rinse and pat them dry.

Pellet Grill Method

  1. Smoke: I smoke the drumsticks on a pellet grill at 300°F for 45 minutes to 1 hour, until they reach 155°F inside. I check them at 45 minutes so they don’t overcook.
  2. Sauce and finish: I brush with BBQ sauce and cook about 10 more minutes on the pellet grill, until they reach 165°F.

Oven Method

  1. Roast: I roast the drumsticks at 425°F on a rack over a foil-lined sheet pan for about 30–40 minutes, until they reach 155°F inside.
  2. Sauce and finish: I brush with BBQ sauce and bake 8–10 more minutes, until they reach 165°F. For a stickier top, I broil 1–2 minutes and watch closely.

Pro tips

  • More tender: Drumsticks stay juicy and get more tender at 175–185°F if you like them fall-off-the-bone.
  • Timing: I check the drumsticks at 45 minutes so they don’t overcook. Pellet grill times vary with drumstick size and the grill.
