Tini's Mac and Cheese

Recipe by Brendan Cunningham at I'm Hungry For That.

Prep time15 min
Cook time30 min
Total45 min
Makes8 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 lb cavatappi pasta
  • 1 lb mozzarella, freshly shredded
  • 1 lb Colby Jack, freshly shredded
  • ½ lb sharp cheddar cheese, freshly shredded
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • ½ teaspoon salt (taste before adding more)
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • 3 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 3 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 1 can (12 oz) evaporated milk
  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard

Steps

  1. I cook the cavatappi al dente in salted water, drain it, and heat the oven to 350°F.
  2. I shred the cheese myself, mix it together, then divide it in half: 20 oz for the sauce and 20 oz for layering. I mix the garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt and pepper in a small bowl.
  3. I melt the unsalted butter over low heat and briefly stir in half the seasoning. I add the flour and cook for 3–5 minutes over low heat, stirring so it does not scorch. I gradually whisk in the evaporated milk until smooth.
  4. I whisk in the cream, remaining seasoning and Dijon, then cook until slightly thickened.
  5. I keep the heat low and gently melt in the sauce half of the cheese, a handful at a time. I taste before adding any more salt, then fold in the pasta.
  6. In a greased 9×13-inch dish, I layer half the pasta, 10 oz of the reserved cheese, the remaining pasta, then the last 10 oz cheese.
  7. I bake for 20–30 minutes, checking at 20 minutes and stopping when melted and bubbling. I broil for 1–2 minutes, watching closely, for a golden top. I garnish with chives if I like.

Original recipe: Tini's Mac and Cheese, I'm Hungry For That. Steps rewritten in my own words.
