Quick Dumpling Laksa

A fast, cozy bowl for busy weeknights.

Quick Dumpling Laksa

Recipe by Nicole at Simple Home Edit.

Prep time5 min
Cook time15 min
Total20 min
Serves4

Times are estimates based on the steps.

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon fresh minced garlic
  • 1 teaspoon fresh minced ginger
  • 2/3 cup (195 g) red curry paste (start with 2 tablespoons for a milder bowl)
  • 14 fl oz (400 ml) coconut milk or coconut cream
  • 4 cups (1 liter) chicken stock
  • 1 teaspoon fish sauce (or tamari or soy sauce)
  • 1 teaspoon sugar (optional), to taste
  • 20 frozen dumplings (veggie, chicken, pork, or shrimp)
  • 1 bunch bok choy or choy sum, halved lengthwise
  • To serve:
  • 1 cup bean sprouts
  • Cilantro leaves
  • Sliced bird's eye chili (optional)
  • Crispy fried shallots
  • Lime wedges

Steps

  1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium heat. Cook the garlic and ginger for about 30 seconds until fragrant.
  2. Stir in the curry paste and cook 1 more minute.
  3. Pour in the coconut milk, stock, fish sauce, and sugar (if using). Stir well.
  4. Bring it to a simmer and gently add the dumplings. Simmer 5–7 minutes until they're cooked through. Add the bok choy for the last 2 minutes.
  5. Ladle into bowls and top with bean sprouts, cilantro, chili, fried shallots, and a squeeze of lime.

Notes

Curry paste heat varies a lot by brand. Start small and add more to taste.

Leftovers keep 3 days in the fridge. Store the broth and dumplings separately so the dumplings don't get soggy.
