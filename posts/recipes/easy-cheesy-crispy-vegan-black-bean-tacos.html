Easy Cheesy Crispy Vegan Black Bean Tacos

Crispy, cheesy, and ready in about 20 minutes with just 6 ingredients.

Easy Cheesy Crispy Vegan Black Bean Tacos

Recipe by Andrew Bernard at Make It Dairy Free.

Prep time10 min
Cook time10 min
Total20 min
Makes6 tacos

Times are estimates based on the steps.

Ingredients

  • 1 (14 oz) can black beans, rinsed and drained
  • 1/2 cup salsa (a thicker one works best)
  • 1–2 teaspoons taco seasoning
  • 6 small flour tortillas
  • 1 cup vegan shredded cheese, divided
  • Vegan butter or oil, for the pan
  • Toppings you like, such as guacamole, cilantro, diced onion, tomatoes, or vegan sour cream

Steps

  1. Blend the black beans, salsa, and taco seasoning in a food processor until smooth, like refried beans.
  2. Heat about 1 teaspoon of oil or vegan butter in a skillet over medium heat. Add the bean mixture and stir for 3–5 minutes until it's hot. Take it off the heat.
  3. Spread about 1/6 of the bean mixture over half of a tortilla. Sprinkle on about 1/6 of the cheese, then fold the tortilla closed. Repeat with the rest.
  4. Wipe out the skillet, add a little more oil or vegan butter, and set it over medium heat. Cook the tacos 1–2 minutes per side until golden and crunchy. Add more oil between batches as needed.
  5. Serve hot with your favorite toppings on the side.

Notes

Keep the filling in a thin layer so the tacos stay crispy and the cheese melts.

No food processor? Mash the beans with a potato masher right in the skillet.

To keep this vegetarian, check the labels: some flour tortillas are made with lard, and some store-bought taco seasoning blends have added animal ingredients.

Leftovers keep in the fridge for up to 5 days. Re-crisp them in a dry skillet or the air fryer.
