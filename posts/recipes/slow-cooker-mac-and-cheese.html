Slow Cooker Mac and Cheese

Creamy slow cooker mac and cheese with cheddar and Velveeta.

Jump to recipe

Sides

Slow Cooker Mac and Cheese

A Sam's Table original.

Prep time15 min
Cook time1½–2 hours
Totalabout 2 hours
MakesA large slow cooker batch
MethodSlow cooker on LOW

Times are estimates based on the steps.

Ingredients

  • 32 oz dried elbow macaroni
  • 4 Tbsp olive oil
  • 4 Tbsp unsalted butter, cubed
  • 2 (12 oz) cans evaporated milk
  • 2½–3 cups milk (whole, almond or soy all work)
  • 2 tsp kosher salt
  • ¾ tsp dried mustard powder
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 5–6 cups shredded cheddar (shred your own)
  • 12–16 oz Velveeta, cubed (start with 12 oz if you want it less dip-like)

Steps

  1. Cook the pasta for 4–5 minutes (still undercooked), then drain.
  2. Spray a 7-quart or larger slow cooker (or use two 6-quart ones if needed) and add the drained pasta.
  3. Add the olive oil and butter and stir to coat.
  4. Add the evaporated milk, milk, seasonings, shredded cheddar and cubed Velveeta. Stir well.
  5. Cover and cook on LOW. Stir at 1 hour, and add more milk then if it looks dry. Cook 30 minutes to 1 hour more, and give it a good stir at the end.

Notes

I made this with almond milk and it came out great.

Start with the lower amounts of milk and Velveeta (2½ cups milk and 12 oz Velveeta). You can always stir in a splash more milk or a few extra ounces of cheese at the end.
