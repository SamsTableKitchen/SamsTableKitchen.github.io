Potato Buns for Smashburgers

Recipe by ALF’s Kitchen on YouTube. This is my favorite potato bun recipe for a smashburger.

Prep time25 min (plus rise)
Bake timeAbout 20 min
Makes8 buns

Ingredients

  • 45 g instant potato flakes
  • 160 g boiling water
  • 80 g warm water
  • 360 g bread flour
  • 7 g instant yeast
  • 10 g granulated sugar
  • 6 g salt
  • 1 large egg (for the dough)
  • 1 large egg (for the egg wash)
  • 28 g unsalted butter, softened

Steps

  1. Make the potatoes: I mix the potato flakes with the 160 g boiling water to make mashed potatoes, then let them cool slightly.
  2. Mix the dough: In a stand mixer, I combine the potatoes, flour, yeast, sugar, salt, 1 egg and the remaining 80 g warm water. I mix on low for 4–5 minutes, until a rough dough forms.
  3. Add butter and knead: I scrape the sides, add the butter, and mix about 3 minutes until it’s incorporated. Then I knead about 15 more minutes, until the dough is sticky and elastic.
  4. First rise: I shape the dough into a ball, seal the seam, and put it in a greased bowl. I cover it and let it rise somewhere warm for 90 minutes.
  5. Shape and proof: I punch the dough down, divide it into 8 equal portions, and shape them into balls. I set them on a parchment-lined sheet, press lightly, cover, and proof about 40 minutes.
  6. Bake: I preheat the oven to 350°F. I brush the buns with egg wash and bake about 20 minutes, until golden brown and they sound hollow when tapped on the bottom. Then I cool them.
