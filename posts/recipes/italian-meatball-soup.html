Easy 30 Minute Italian Meatball Soup

Mini meatballs, veggies, and small pasta in a marinara broth. A cozy one-pot weeknight dinner.

Easy 30 Minute Italian Meatball Soup

Recipe by Carly's Cookbook on Chefadora.

Prep time10 min
Cook time20 min
Total30 min
Serves4

Times are estimates based on the steps.

Ingredients

  • 16 oz mini meatballs
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 24 oz marinara or vodka sauce
  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • 1 tablespoon Italian seasoning
  • 1 tablespoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon red pepper flakes
  • 2 cups uncooked small pasta (like shells)
  • 2 cups fresh spinach
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan, plus more for serving
  • 1/4 cup heavy cream (optional)
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Steps

  1. Warm the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion, carrots, and celery with a pinch of salt and cook for 6–8 minutes, until soft.
  2. Stir in the garlic and cook for about 30 seconds, just until fragrant.
  3. Add the marinara, chicken broth, Italian seasoning, garlic powder, and red pepper flakes. Bring it up to a simmer.
  4. Drop in the mini meatballs and simmer for 8–10 minutes.
  5. Add the pasta and cook it in the soup for the time on the package, until just tender.
  6. Stir in the spinach and Parmesan until the spinach wilts.
  7. Stir in the heavy cream if you want it creamier. Taste and add salt and pepper as needed.
  8. Ladle into bowls and top with more Parmesan.

Notes

Saving leftovers? Cook the pasta separately and add it to each bowl. Otherwise it keeps soaking up the broth and gets very soft.

Regular-size meatballs work too. Simmer them a little longer so they're hot all the way through.

Vegetable broth works in place of chicken broth.

Original recipe: Easy 30 Minute Italian Meatball Soup, Carly's Cookbook on Chefadora. Steps rewritten in my own words.
