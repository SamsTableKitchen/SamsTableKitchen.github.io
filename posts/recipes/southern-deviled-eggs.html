Southern Deviled Eggs

Recipe by Malcom Reed at HowToBBQRight.

Prep time20 min
Cook time20 min
TotalAbout 45 min, plus chilling
Makes24 halves

Ingredients

  • 12 large eggs
  • ½ cup mayo
  • 2 teaspoons sweet pickle relish
  • 2 teaspoons yellow mustard
  • 1 teaspoon apple cider vinegar
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • Garnish
  • Optional: sliced pickled jalapeños
  • Fresh chives, finely chopped
  • Sweet paprika

Steps

  1. Cook the eggs on the stove. Arrange them in a single layer in a large pot and add cold water until they sit under about an inch of water. Bring the pot to a boil over medium-high heat. As soon as it boils, turn off the heat, cover the pot, and let the eggs sit for 10 minutes.
    Alternative: smoker method

    Instead of boiling, set a pellet grill or smoker to 325°F and smoke the eggs 30–40 minutes, then remove them from the smoker and place them in an ice bath, then peel under running water and continue with the filling.

  2. Cool the eggs under cold water, then peel them while the water is still running so the shells come off more easily.
  3. Cut each egg in half from end to end and scoop the yolks into a bowl. Mash the yolks with a fork until they're crumbly. Stir in the mayo, sweet pickle relish, yellow mustard, apple cider vinegar, salt, and pepper until the filling is evenly mixed.
  4. Spoon about a teaspoon of filling into each egg white half.
  5. Finish with a little chopped fresh chive and a dusting of sweet paprika. Optional: sliced pickled jalapeños. Cover them loosely and refrigerate until you're ready to put them out.

Notes

This is one of the recipes I like, and it came out great.

Recipe by Malcom Reed at HowToBBQRight.
