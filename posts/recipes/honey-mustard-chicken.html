Honey Mustard Chicken

Sticky, golden chicken thighs roasted in a quick honey mustard marinade.

Honey Mustard Chicken

Recipe by Bob Morano.

🧪 Kitchen-test in progress: I haven't made this one yet, so let me know how it turns out!

Prep time10 min
Cook time25 to 30 min
ServesAbout 4

Ingredients

  • Marinade:
  • 3 to 4 garlic cloves, grated or minced
  • ⅓ cup honey
  • ⅓ cup mustard
  • Pinch of kosher salt
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • ¼ teaspoon onion powder
  • 2 to 3 teaspoons vinegar
  • About ⅓ to ½ cup oil
  • Chicken:
  • 1½ to 2 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs

Steps

  1. Make the marinade: Heat the oven to 450°F (230°C). Whisk the garlic, honey, mustard, salt, pepper, onion powder, vinegar and oil together in a large bowl.
  2. Coat the chicken: Add the thighs and toss until fully coated. Marinating is optional; if you do, cover and refrigerate for up to several hours.
  3. Arrange: Lay the thighs in a single layer in a baking dish or on a sheet pan, and pour any extra marinade over them.
  4. Roast: Roast for 25 to 30 minutes, until the thickest piece reads 165°F (74°C) on a meat thermometer.
  5. Caramelize: If the chicken is cooked but not caramelized, broil for about 1 minute, watching closely so the honey doesn't burn.
  6. Serve: Spoon the pan juices over the chicken before serving.

Pro tips

  • Food safety: Always check the thickest thigh with a thermometer; 165°F (74°C) is the safe minimum for poultry.
  • Easy cleanup: Line the sheet pan with foil, since the honey caramelizes onto the pan.
