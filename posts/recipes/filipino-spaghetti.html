Filipino Sweet Spaghetti

Sweet, savory, and a party favorite.

Filipino Sweet Spaghetti

Recipe by MG Sanchez at Hungry Huy, with my own tweaks.

Prep time5 min
Cook time30 min
Total35 min
Serves8

Times are estimates based on the steps.

Ingredients

  • For the sauce:
  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
  • 1 medium yellow onion, chopped
  • 5 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 lb ground pork
  • 1 jar Prego Fire Roasted Tomato Italian sauce (or 16 fl oz canned tomato sauce)
  • 4 fl oz banana ketchup
  • 1 tablespoon dark brown sugar
  • 1 tablespoon fish sauce
  • 1/2 tablespoon onion powder
  • 1/2 tablespoon garlic powder
  • 5 hot dogs, sliced on an angle (optional)
  • 2 teaspoons liquid smoke (I use Figaro Mesquite)
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • Velveeta or cheddar cheese, for topping
  • For the pasta:
  • 1 lb dried spaghetti
  • 10 cups water
  • 2 teaspoons salt

Steps

  1. Heat the oil in a large pan over medium heat. Cook the onion 3–5 minutes until soft, then add the garlic for 30 seconds.
  2. Add the ground pork and cook 5–7 minutes until browned.
  3. Stir in the tomato sauce, banana ketchup, fish sauce, brown sugar, onion powder, and garlic powder. Bring to a boil, then lower to medium-low and simmer 5 minutes to thicken.
  4. Add the hot dogs (if using) and simmer 5 more minutes. Stir in the liquid smoke near the end, then taste and add salt and pepper.
  5. Meanwhile, boil the water with the salt and cook the spaghetti according to the package. Drain, saving a little pasta water in case the sauce needs thinning. Don't rinse the pasta.
  6. Plate the spaghetti, ladle the sauce on top, and finish with cheese. Serve right away.

Notes

You can substitute ground turkey or ground beef for the pork if you prefer.

My tweaks

This is my personal favorite version. The tweaks below are mine; the original recipe is by MG Sanchez at Hungry Huy.

Fish sauce: I bump it up to 1 tablespoon. It doesn't make it taste like fish at all. It just adds a deep, savory flavor.

Onion and garlic powder: I add 1/2 tablespoon of each when the sauce goes in.

Sauce: I usually make my own sauce, but when I need a quick meal, a jar of Prego Fire Roasted marinara is the way to go. The whole thing comes together in under 1 hour.

Liquid smoke: I add 2 teaspoons toward the end. Be careful, it's very concentrated and strong, so start small and taste. Liquid smoke gets a bad rap, but it's real smoke, not a lab chemical. It's made by burning wood like hickory or mesquite, capturing the smoke, cooling it so it condenses into a liquid, and filtering it. It's basically smoke in a bottle.

Hot dogs are optional: If you don't like them or don't have them, skip them. The ground meat still makes it a great meal.

Original recipe: Filipino Spaghetti (w/ Sweet Spaghetti Sauce), Hungry Huy. Steps rewritten in my own words.
