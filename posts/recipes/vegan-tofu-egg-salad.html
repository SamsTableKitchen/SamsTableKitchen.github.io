Vegan Tofu Egg Salad

Recipe by Sarah Sullivan at Sarah's Vegan Kitchen.

For the mayo, I use my homemade Vegan Mayonnaise.

Prep time10 min (plus 30 min to press)
Chill1–2 hr (optional)
TotalAbout 40 min
Serves5

Times are estimates based on the steps.

Ingredients

  • 1 (14 oz) block firm or extra-firm tofu, divided
  • 2 green onions, finely sliced (or about 2 tablespoons finely diced red or white onion)
  • 1–2 tablespoons fresh dill, finely chopped
  • 1 stalk celery, finely diced
  • 1/2 cup vegan mayo (I use my homemade vegan mayo)
  • 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard, to taste
  • 1/2 teaspoon black salt (kala namak)
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • Salt, to taste
  • 1/8 teaspoon turmeric (optional, for color)
  • Juice of 1/2 lemon (optional)

Steps

  1. Drain the tofu and press it for at least 30 minutes to squeeze out the water. Wrap it in a clean towel and set something heavy on top, like a cutting board with a couple of cans. Don't skip this, or the salad turns watery.
  2. Put half of the tofu in a bowl with the mayo, mustard, and turmeric. Mash it with a fork into a thick, creamy dressing.
  3. Roughly chop the other half of the tofu into small pieces, like chopped egg whites. Add it to the bowl with the celery, green onions, dill, black salt, and pepper. Gently fold everything together.
  4. Taste it and adjust. Add more dill, mustard, a squeeze of lemon, or a pinch of salt if it needs it.
  5. Eat it right away, or chill it for 1–2 hours so the flavors come together. Serve it on bread, over greens, or in lettuce wraps.
My tip

I like to add a little nutritional yeast to help with the taste.

Notes

Black salt is what gives this its eggy flavor. Look for it at Indian markets or online. If you can't find it, use regular salt.

Keeps in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 5 days, but it's best in the first 3.

Original recipe: Vegan Egg Salad (Tofu Egg Salad), Sarah's Vegan Kitchen. Steps rewritten in my own words.
