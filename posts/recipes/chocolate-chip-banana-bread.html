Chocolate Chip Banana Bread

Adapted from Natasha Kravchuk of Natasha’s Kitchen. I leave out the nuts and add mini chocolate chips.

Prep time10 min
Bake time55–60 min
Makes1 loaf (10 servings)

Ingredients

  • 3 very ripe medium/large bananas (about 1½ cups mashed)
  • ½ cup unsalted butter, softened
  • ¾ cup granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs, lightly beaten
  • 1½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon vanilla extract
  • ½ cup raisins (optional, as in the original recipe)
  • ½ cup mini chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Prepare: I heat the oven to 350°F and grease and flour a loaf pan about 9¼ × 5¼ × 2¾ inches.
  2. Cream: I beat the softened butter and sugar together until creamy.
  3. Add bananas: I mash the bananas with a fork, leaving a little texture, then mix them into the butter mixture with the beaten eggs.
  4. Add dry ingredients: In another bowl, I whisk together the flour, baking soda and salt, then mix them into the banana batter.
  5. Fold in: I stir in the vanilla, mini chocolate chips and raisins if using, then transfer the batter to the prepared pan.
  6. Bake and cool: I bake at 350°F for 55–60 minutes, until a toothpick in the center comes out clean of wet batter. I let the loaf sit in the pan for 10 minutes before moving it to a wire rack to cool.

Pro tips

  • My note: I skip the nuts and add ½ cup mini chocolate chips, but I always end up adding more.
