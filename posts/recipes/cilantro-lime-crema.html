Cilantro Lime Crema

Recipe by Jeanine Donofrio at Love and Lemons.

Prep time5 min
Total5 min
Makes6 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 cup whole milk Greek yogurt or sour cream
  • 2 tablespoons mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon fresh lime juice
  • 1 small garlic clove
  • ½ teaspoon sea salt
  • ¼ teaspoon onion powder
  • ½ cup fresh cilantro

Steps

  1. Blend the yogurt, mayo, lime juice, garlic, salt, and onion powder in a food processor until creamy.
  2. Add the cilantro and pulse until smooth with little green flecks. Taste and adjust.

Original recipe: Cilantro Lime Crema, Love and Lemons. Steps rewritten in my own words.
