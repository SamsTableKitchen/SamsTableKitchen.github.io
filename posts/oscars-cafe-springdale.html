Restaurant Review · Springdale, UT

Oscar's Cafe

Burgers & Tex-Mex near Zion

★★★★★
Oscar's Cafe
948 Zion Park Blvd, Springdale, UT 84767
Rating: 5/5

Only 5 minutes from Zion (shuttle stop #5), Oscar’s is a must-try. I had the Murder Burger — a half-pound garlic burger with pepper jack, cheddar, bacon, and all the fixings — and it was one of the best burgers I’ve ever had, with great prices too. I also tried the pork enchiladas and they were fantastic. Service was friendly and fast. Definitely worth stopping.

What we ordered

Murder Burger · Pork Enchiladas

About the place

Owner-operated cafe in Springdale, at the mouth of Zion National Park, open since 1991. It started as a small walk-up-and-order spot and grew into a full-service restaurant run by Hank and Kathy Moore, with a covered patio facing Zion's red rock peaks. It serves breakfast, lunch and dinner, with a mix of American and Mexican-inspired dishes: big half-pound garlic burgers like the Murder Burger, breakfast burritos, pancakes and omelets.

What other diners say: Reviewers on Tripadvisor often praise the generous portions, friendly service, breakfasts and patio views. Many say it's one of the better values in a pricey tourist town, while a few found certain dishes underwhelming.

Sources: the restaurant's website, Roadtrippers and Tripadvisor. Summaries are paraphrased, not quotes.
