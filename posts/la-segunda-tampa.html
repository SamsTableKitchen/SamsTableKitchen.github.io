Restaurant Review · Tampa, FL

La Segunda Bakery and Cafe

★★★★★
La Segunda Bakery and Cafe
2512 N 15th St, Tampa, FL 33605
Rating: 5/5

Came for the Cuban sandwich and stayed staring at the dessert case. La Segunda has been baking authentic Cuban bread daily since 1915, and the sandwich lived up to that reputation, easily the best Cuban I've had after trying a few others around Florida. The bread had that perfect crackle outside and soft crumb inside.

What I didn't expect was how stacked the pastry case would be. There were custard horns and cream puffs dusted in powdered sugar, guava cheese and apple turnovers, flan cups, strawberry shortcake, NY cheesecake with fruit, carrot cake, red velvet, Napoleons, and a piña colada cake with toasted coconut and a cherry on top. It felt less like a side display and more like half the reason locals keep showing up.

Parking is tight but manageable (street spots work for a quick stop). No seating inside, so grab-and-go or order ahead. If you're in Tampa for Cuban bread done right, and a dessert case that makes you renegotiate your order, this is the stop.

About the place

La Segunda Central Bakery was founded in Ybor City in 1915 by Juan Moré, a Catalan-born baker who came to love Cuban bread while serving in Cuba during the Spanish-American War. It has stayed in the Moré family for four generations and is now run by his great-grandson, Copeland Moré. The bakery still makes its Cuban bread by hand, pressing a fresh palmetto leaf along the top of each loaf to create the signature split, and it supplies bread to restaurants all over the Tampa Bay area. The Ybor City bakery on 15th Street is takeout only and is open 6:30 a.m. to 3 p.m. daily (checked October 2026); there are also cafes in Seminole Heights, on Kennedy Boulevard and in St. Petersburg.

What other diners say: Reviewers on Tripadvisor and Yelp, along with The Infatuation, single out the Cuban sandwich and the fresh bread above everything else, and many also recommend the guava and cheese pastries and the Cuban coffee. Common complaints are lines at breakfast and lunch, tight parking, and no place to sit at the Ybor location. Several suggest ordering ahead for pickup.

What to order: The Cuban sandwich, a loaf of Cuban bread to take home, and a guava and cheese pastry.

Sources: La Segunda's website, The Infatuation, Tripadvisor and Yelp. Summaries are paraphrased, not quotes.
