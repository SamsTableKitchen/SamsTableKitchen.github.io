Classic Chicken Salad

Recipe by Lisa Bryan at Downshiftology.

Prep time10 min
Cook time15 min
Total25 min
Servings6
Calories524 per serving*

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds boneless skinless chicken breasts
  • 1 cup red grapes (quartered)
  • 2 celery ribs (diced)
  • 3 green onions (green and white parts) (sliced)
  • 1 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
  • ½ cup sliced raw almonds
  • 2 tablespoon finely chopped parsley
  • 1 tablespoon finely chopped tarragon
  • 1 lemon (juiced (about 3 tablespoons))
  • kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper (to taste)

Steps

  1. Put the chicken in a wide pan, cover with cold salted water by about an inch, and bring to a gentle simmer.
  2. Turn the heat to low, cover, and cook until the chicken is done in the middle. Let it cool.
  3. Dice the chicken and put it in a big bowl.
  4. Add the grapes, celery, green onion, mayo, mustard, almonds, herbs, and lemon juice. Season and chill.

*Calories are Downshiftology's own estimate from the original recipe, not mine.
