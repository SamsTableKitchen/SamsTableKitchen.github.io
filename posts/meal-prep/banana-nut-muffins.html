Banana Nut Muffins (Almond Flour)

Recipe by Lisa Bryan at Downshiftology.

Prep time5 min
Cook time25 min
Total30 min
Servings12
Calories251.2 per serving*

Ingredients

  • 2-3 ripe bananas (approx 1 1/4 cups mashed)
  • 2 cups almond flour
  • 1/2 cup tapioca flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/4 cup honey
  • 1/4 cup butter or coconut oil (melted)
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 cup chopped pecans or walnuts (with extra for topping)

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F and line a muffin pan with 12 liners.
  2. Mash the bananas in a big bowl, then add everything else except the nuts.
  3. Mix with a hand mixer for about 30 seconds, then stir in the nuts.
  4. Split the batter between the cups and sprinkle a few more nuts on top.
  5. Bake 22 to 25 minutes, until golden and a toothpick comes out clean.

*Calories are Downshiftology's own estimate from the original recipe, not mine.
