Budget-Friendly Week

Built on oats, beans, lentils, rice, and ground meat. Cook on Sunday, eat well Monday to Friday.

Most recipes make 4 to 6 servings. If you're cooking for more people, double the lunch and dinner recipes. Anything past 4 days is best frozen on Sunday and thawed the night before.

Sunday prep, step by step

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F and bake the oatmeal (about 45 minutes).
  2. At the same time, start the lentil soup in one pot and the taco soup in another.
  3. Cook a pot of brown rice for the buffalo bowls.
  4. Make the buffalo chicken and beans while the soups simmer.
  5. Toss the chickpea salad last, then portion everything. Soups freeze well if you have extra.
