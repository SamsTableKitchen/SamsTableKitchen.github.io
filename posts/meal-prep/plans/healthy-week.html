Healthy Week

Lots of veggies, beans, and lean protein. Cook on Sunday, eat well Monday to Friday.

Most recipes make 4 to 6 servings. If you're cooking for more people, double the lunch and dinner recipes. Anything past 4 days is best frozen on Sunday and thawed the night before.

Sunday prep, step by step

  1. Start the lentil soup first since it simmers the longest (about 45 minutes).
  2. While it simmers, heat the oven to 425°F and get the fajitas on a sheet pan (20 minutes).
  3. When the fajitas come out, drop the oven to 400°F and roast the veggies for the quinoa bowls. Cook the quinoa on the stove if you don't have it ready.
  4. While things roast, make the chickpea salad and stir up 5 jars of overnight oats.
  5. Let everything cool, then portion into containers. Keep the arugula and tortillas separate.
