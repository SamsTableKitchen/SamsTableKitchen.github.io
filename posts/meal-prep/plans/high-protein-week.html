High-Protein Week

Protein-packed picks with chicken, turkey, and eggs. Cook on Sunday, eat well Monday to Friday.

Most recipes make 4 to 6 servings. If you're cooking for more people, double the lunch and dinner recipes. Anything past 4 days is best frozen on Sunday and thawed the night before.

Sunday prep, step by step

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F. Roast the sweet potatoes and bacon for the casserole while you brown the sausage.
  2. Build the casserole and bake it. Meanwhile, start the turkey chili on the stove (it simmers 30 minutes).
  3. Bump the oven to 425°F. Bake the honey sriracha chicken and broccoli, then the Greek sheet pan chicken.
  4. While the Greek chicken roasts, roll the meatballs and cook the couscous. Bake or air fry the meatballs.
  5. Chop the cucumber salad, cool everything, and portion. Freeze two servings of chili if you want them later.

Grocery list

Everything for all 5 recipes, sorted by store section. Check your pantry first; salt, pepper, oil, and spices you already have can be skipped. Toppings are up to you.
