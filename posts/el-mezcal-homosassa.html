Restaurant Review · Homosassa, FL

El Mezcal

Mexican Restaurant

★★★★★
El Mezcal | Mexican Restaurant
4522 S Suncoast Blvd, Homosassa, FL 34446
Rating: 5/5

Really enjoyed our visit to El Mezcal! We came in on Sunday afternoon and it wasn’t busy at all. The service was great — our water was always full, and we never had to wait for chips and salsa.

We ordered the Chipotle Shrimp and the Shrimp Chimichangas, and for the chimichangas we tried them soft instead of fried. That was a different way to have them, but they were very, very good! Everything was super flavorful with a nice smoky chipotle flavor without being overly spicy. The cheese sauce on the shrimp chimichangas was especially good.

I also really enjoyed the guacamole, and it was a nice bonus that it was included with the chimichangas. Both dishes were $19.99, which I thought was a great value for the quality and portion.

What we ordered

Chipotle Shrimp · Shrimp Chimichangas (soft)

About the place

Mexican restaurant on S Suncoast Blvd in Homosassa, with a full bar. The menu covers Mexican-American favorites like fajitas, burritos, chimichangas, quesadillas and enchiladas, plus seafood dishes such as chipotle shrimp. Guacamole is made at the table.

What other diners say: Reviewers on Google and Yelp often praise the fresh, hot food, friendly staff, fast service and welcoming atmosphere. Some mention long waits at busy times and occasional inconsistency.

Sources: Apple Maps (Yelp), Restaurantji and Foodeist (Google reviews). Summaries are paraphrased, not quotes.
