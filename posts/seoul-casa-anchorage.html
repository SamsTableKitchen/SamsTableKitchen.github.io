Restaurant Review · Anchorage, AK

Seoul Casa

Korean Mexican Fusion

★★★★★
Seoul Casa
601 E Dimond Blvd Ste 6, Anchorage, AK 99515
Rating: 5/5

Seoul Casa is an absolute gem! The fusion of Korean and Mexican flavors is executed perfectly. I ordered the bulgogi egg rolls, which were crispy and packed with delicious, savory flavor. The kalbi beef enchiladas and quesadilla were both outstanding—tender, flavorful, and filling. The mac salad was the perfect side to round out the meal. Every dish was well-prepared and full of unique, delightful flavors. Highly recommend this spot for anyone looking to try something different and amazing!

What we ordered

Bulgogi Egg Rolls · Kalbi Beef Enchiladas · Quesadilla with Mac Salad

About the place

Korean-Mexican fusion restaurant in South Anchorage that opened in summer 2018, co-owned by Andrew Cho and Ronald Tzou. It was featured on Food Network's Diners, Drive-Ins and Dives in 2022. Signature dishes include the Kimchirito (a kimchi fried rice burrito), K-Town nachos topped with kalbi beef, and bulgogi egg rolls.

What other diners say: Reviewers on Tripadvisor and Yelp often praise how well the Korean and Mexican flavors work together, especially the Kimchirito, kalbi beef and kimchi fried rice, along with the friendly service. A few mention slower service when busy.

Sources: the Anchorage Daily News, Alaska's News Source, Tripadvisor and Apple Maps (Yelp). Summaries are paraphrased, not quotes.
